隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 20 日 · 适用于镜藏 Lite 的 Mac 与原生 iPhone 应用

本应用由个人开发者提供，销售方身份以 App Store 产品页面为准。有关隐私或人工反馈删除的请求，请联系 kenapple9527@gmail.com。

本机资料与系统权限

媒体、片段、标签、收藏、播放记录及资料库设置保存在你的设备或你选择的存储位置。应用没有镜藏云端账户，开发者不运营接收用户资料库或原片的云存储，不自动上传这些内容。

Mac 可以引用外部原片，也可按你选择的操作复制或移动文件；iPhone 导入会保存所选媒体的本机副本。照片和文件选择器提供你主动选择的项目；iCloud 项目可能需要系统先下载。

本地网络权限用于发现、连接可信设备并同步或传输媒体。Face ID／Touch ID 由系统完成身份验证，应用不读取或保存生物识别数据。资料库密码是应用内访问保护，不代表数据库或原片另加了文件加密。

可信设备与网络传输

设备身份、可信设备名称、连接地址及同步状态保存在本机；配对访问凭证保存在系统钥匙串。你授权连接后，资料库变更及请求的媒体会传给相应可信设备。

局域网连接使用 HTTP，请在可信网络中使用。远程连接可使用你另行安装及配置的 Tailscale；它的账户、网络协调和可能的中继由该服务及你的网络设置管理。镜藏不内置 VPN 服务，也不代你管理 Tailscale 账户。网络提供方和操作系统可能根据各自政策处理连接数据。

缓存、备份与删除

应用会生成缩略图、播放缓存、兼容播放副本和传输暂存文件。可重新生成的缓存与暂存可能被排除在系统设备备份之外；用户资料和原片是否实际备份取决于系统、账户与存储设置。请自行保留重要资料的备份。

你可以在应用中清理缓存、管理媒体、移除本机资料库或撤销设备信任。具体移除范围以操作确认提示为准；清理缓存不会删除托管原片。从某台设备移除本机资料库副本不会自动删除其他设备副本。若要撤销对方的访问，也需在相应设备取消信任。钥匙串记录不应被假定会随卸载自动删除。

购买与第三方服务

如所用版本提供 App 内购买，付款、订阅管理和退款由 Apple 处理。应用通过 Apple 的购买验证信息确认功能权益；开发者不接收你的银行卡号或 Apple 账户密码。购买权益与资料库、设备配对信任分别管理。Apple 服务的数据处理受其隐私政策和你的系统设置约束。

App Store 版本由 Apple 提供更新。其他分发渠道的 Mac 版本可能在你检查更新时连接其公开更新源及托管服务，这些服务会接收提供下载所需的网络请求信息。

诊断与主动反馈

应用没有广告、第三方行为分析或跨应用追踪，不自动向开发者发送诊断报告。本机和系统日志可能包含版本、错误代码及连接、导入或播放状态。

如果你主动发送邮件、诊断或附件，开发者会收到你提供的联系方式、问题说明及附带内容，用于处理支持请求。诊断可能包含设备／资料库标识、版本、同步状态和存储统计；发送前请检查，不要提供密码、配对码、令牌或私人原片。

人工反馈只在处理请求及履行适用记录义务所需的期间保留，不用于广告或出售。你可通过支持邮箱请求删除不再需要保留的反馈；依法必须保留的信息除外。电子邮件由邮箱服务商传输和保存，受其服务政策约束。

本网站与政策更新

本网站不设置行为分析或广告追踪代码，由 GitHub Pages 托管；托管方可能为提供及保护网站处理访问日志，请参阅 GitHub 隐私声明。应用新增数据处理方式时，本政策会随之更新；请以本页日期及对应版本的说明为准。

Privacy Policy

Updated October 20, 2026 · Mirror Lite for Mac and native iPhone

Mirror Lite is provided by an individual developer. The seller identity appears on the App Store product page. For privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence, contact kenapple9527@gmail.com.

Local data and permissions

Media, clips, tags, collections, playback history, and library settings are stored on your devices or storage locations you select. There is no Mirror cloud account or developer-operated cloud storage for your libraries or originals. The app does not automatically upload this content to the developer.

Mac can reference external originals or copy or move files according to your chosen operation. iPhone imports save local copies. System photo and file pickers provide the items you select; iCloud items may require a system download first.

Local network access discovers and connects trusted devices for sync and media transfer. Face ID and Touch ID authentication is performed by the operating system; the app cannot read or store your biometric data. Library passwords control access within the app and do not mean that the app separately encrypts your database or original files.

Trusted devices and transfers

Device identity, trusted device names, connection addresses, and sync status are stored locally. Pairing credentials are stored in the system Keychain. After you authorize a connection, library changes and requested media are sent to the relevant trusted device.

Local connections use HTTP and should be used on trusted networks. For remote connections, you may separately install and configure Tailscale. Its account, network coordination, and possible relaying are governed by that service and your network configuration. Mirror Lite does not include a VPN service or manage your Tailscale account. Network providers and the operating system may process connection data under their own policies.

Caches, backups, and deletion

The app generates thumbnails, playback caches, compatible playback copies, and temporary transfer files. Regenerable caches and temporary files may be excluded from system device backups. Whether user data and originals are actually backed up depends on your system, account, and storage settings. Keep independent backups of important data.

You can clear caches, manage media, remove local libraries, or revoke device trust. The operation's confirmation explains its scope. Clearing caches does not delete managed originals. Removing a local library copy does not automatically remove copies on other devices. To revoke a peer's access, cancel trust on the relevant device too. Keychain entries must not be assumed to disappear when the app is uninstalled.

Purchases and other services

If your version offers in-app purchases, Apple handles payment, subscription management, and refunds. The app uses Apple-verified purchase information to determine feature access. The developer does not receive your payment card number or Apple Account password. Purchase access is separate from library data and device pairing. Apple's services are governed by its privacy policy and your system settings.

App Store versions receive updates through Apple. Mac versions from other distribution channels may contact their public update feed and hosting providers when you check for updates; those providers receive the network requests needed to deliver downloads.

Diagnostics and voluntary support

The app contains no advertising, third-party behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking, and does not automatically send diagnostic reports to the developer. Local and system logs may include versions, error codes, and connection, import, or playback status.

When you voluntarily send email, diagnostics, or attachments, the developer receives the contact details, description, and content you provide to handle your support request. Diagnostics may include device or library identifiers, versions, sync status, and storage statistics. Review them before sending; do not include passwords, pairing codes, tokens, or private originals.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle requests and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations, and is not sold or used for advertising. You may request deletion through the support email, except where retention is legally required. Email is transmitted and stored by the email provider under its policies.

This website and policy changes

This site has no advertising or analytics tracking code and is hosted on GitHub Pages. The host may process access logs to operate and protect the site; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. This policy will be updated when the app's data processing changes. Refer to this page's date and the information for your version.
